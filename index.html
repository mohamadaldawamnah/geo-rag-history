Interactive History Explorer

Explore nearby historical places, ask concise contextual questions, and keep cached regions ready when APIs are unstable. Press Start to launch the app.

Step 1
Click on the map or search a place to load nearby landmarks.
Step 2
Open landmark details to read tags and available history text.
Step 3
Use the RAG tab to ask short questions, optionally with a year.
Step 4
Use Cache Region, then open the Cached tab to reload saved areas and see their radius circle.
Tip: cached areas are highlighted with a red translucent radius.
